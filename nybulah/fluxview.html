__TITLE__: flux as an image

Angle runs left to right from the index (or sector 0 without one); each halftrack is a band, one strip per revolution. Lightness: flux transitions per bit cell (each spread over a quarter cell, the read circuit's clock). Hue: cell length against the track's standard zone beyond its measurement bound, blue shorter, orange longer, saturating at one zone step; greyer where revolutions disagree. Dots: no flux inferred from bits (runs of 3+ zeros, ones between taken as read noise). Green: a decode slip starts. Wheel zooms, drag pans, click picks the track for the panels.
